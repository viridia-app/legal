Delete Your Account

✦

You may request the deletion of your Viridia account and all associated personal data at any time. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Upon confirmation, the following data will be permanently removed:

Your account credentials and profile information
All journal entries and healing log history
Your saved cabinet (favorited remedies)
Any preferences or settings associated with your account

What is retained

Anonymized, non-identifiable usage data may be retained for analytics purposes in accordance with our Privacy Policy. No data that can identify you personally will be kept after deletion.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In-app: Go to Profile → Delete Account within the Viridia app.
Option 2 — By email: Send a request to [email protected] with the subject line "Delete My Account" and include the email address associated with your account.
