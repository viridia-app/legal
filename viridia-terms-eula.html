01 What Viridia Is

Viridia is a natural remedy reference library. It is a tool for education and personal research — not a medical service, not a diagnostic system, and not a substitute for professional healthcare.

The app provides information about herbs, natural modalities, and traditional health practices. That information is drawn from historical use, published research, and traditional herbal frameworks. It is presented plainly, with citations where available, and without pharmaceutical framing or influencer branding.

It is a cabinet. A codex. Not a clinic.

02 What Viridia Is Not

Using Viridia does not create a patient-provider relationship of any kind. Nothing in the app — no remedy entry, no Body Oracle result, no preparation guide, no healing journal prompt — constitutes medical advice, a medical diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation.

Viridia does not know your health history, your current medications, your allergies, or your circumstances. It cannot. Do not use it as though it does.

If you are dealing with a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are taking prescription medications, or are uncertain whether an herb or modality is appropriate for you, consult a qualified healthcare provider before using anything you find here.

03 The Warning Label Flag

Certain remedies in Viridia carry a warning label designation. This flag indicates that the remedy has a meaningful caution — hormonal activity, CNS effects, known drug interactions, or risks that change significantly with dose or individual factors.

The flag is not a prohibition. It is information. Read it. Take it seriously. If you are in a flagged category — on thyroid medication, managing an estrogen-sensitive condition, pregnant — give those entries more scrutiny, not less.

Viridia flags these entries in good faith for educational purposes. What you do with that information is your decision.

You are responsible for how you use Viridia. The app is designed for adults who are capable of reading information critically and making their own decisions. It is written to that standard.

05 Accuracy & Updates

Viridia makes a genuine effort to present accurate, well-sourced information. Herbal research is an active field. Traditional frameworks evolve. Our understanding of mechanisms improves.

We update entries as the evidence warrants. We do not guarantee that every entry reflects the current state of research at every moment. Where evidence is limited, we say so. Where evidence is strong, we say that too.

If you find an error — a wrong citation, a mechanism misstated, a preparation with a safety issue we missed — tell us. We would rather know.
